# SRAG / SIVEP-Gripe — data dictionary

Generated by [`tools/build_srag_dictionary.py`](../../tools/build_srag_dictionary.py) from two sources, because neither is sufficient alone: the Ministry of Health's official dictionary (`dicionario-de-dados-2019-a-2025.pdf`, dated 2023-05-25) defines the fields and their coded values, and the published parquet files say which columns actually exist. **Where the two disagree the data wins**, and the disagreement is recorded below.

Field labels and coded values are kept in Portuguese: they are the official names on the notification form, and translating them would break the link to the source.

## Reading this table

- **Class** is the official form's: *Obrigatório* blocks the record if absent, *Essencial* is needed for the investigation but does not block, *Interno* is filled by the system itself, *Opcional* only when applicable. It explains **why** a field is missing.
- **Year columns** give the share of records with a non-null value, measured from the parquet column statistics.
- ⚠️ marks a field that is a *consequence* of the outcome: using it to predict `EVOLUCAO` is leakage, not prediction.

> **Blank is not `9-Ignorado`.** They are different things, and the distinction is this dataset's central trap. A blank is an absence of record; `9` is an explicit record of not knowing. On the comorbidities, treating blank as `2-Não` inverts the prevalence — in 2021 `CARDIOPATI` is blank in 56.1% of records and, among those filled, 62.5% say *Sim*; treating blank as *Não* would report 27.5% with heart disease instead of 62.5%.

## Where the official dictionary and the published data disagree

**11 columns exist in the parquet and are not defined in the official PDF.** The published dictionary is dated 2023-05-25 and did not follow every revision of the form:

- `COD_IDADE`
- `NM_UN_INTE`
- `HISTO_VGM`
- `PAIS_VGM`
- `CO_PS_VGM`
- `LO_PS_VGM`
- `DT_VGM`
- `DT_RT_VGM`
- `PCR_SARS2`
- `VG_CODEST`
- `REINF`

The PDF also spells `CO-DETEC`, `FAB_COV1` and `FAB_COV2` where the data publishes `CO_DETEC`, `FAB_COV_1` and `FAB_COV_2`; the generator anchors on both spellings.

## Fields that leak the outcome

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| `DT_EVOLUCA` | outcome date — only exists once the outcome has happened |
| `DT_ENCERRA` | case closure date |
| `UTI` | ICU admission follows from severity and is contemporaneous with the outcome |
| `DT_ENTUTI` | same, and posterior to admission |
| `DT_SAIDUTI` | same |
| `SUPORT_VEN` | ventilatory support follows from severity |
| `CLASSI_FIN` | final case classification, assigned at closure |
| `CRITERIO` | closure criterion |
| `VG_ENC` | genomic-surveillance closure |

None of these should enter as a predictor of `EVOLUCAO` unless the module states explicitly that it is measuring association, not prediction.
